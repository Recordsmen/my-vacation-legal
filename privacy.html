Privacy Policy

My Vacation · Last updated: 1 November 2026

My Vacation lets you take three photos a day during a vacation and keeps them sealed until it ends. It is built to work without an account and without a server. This page explains what that means for your data.

Your photos stay on your phone

Photos you take or pick, vacation names, dates and reminder times are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not upload them, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

On iPhone, the app's data is part of your iCloud or computer backup, under Apple's terms. On Android it is left out of the Google cloud backup (which is too small for a vacation of photos) and moves to a new phone only with a direct phone-to-phone transfer.

Camera and photo library

  • Camera — to take your morning, day and evening photos inside the app.
  • Picking from the gallery — uses the system photo picker: the app only receives the photo you choose. It reads that photo's capture date to check it was taken during the current slot.
  • Saving to Photos — on iOS the app asks for add-only access to save a finished collage. It never reads your library.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No push service is involved.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Firebase Crashlytics (Google): the device model, OS version, app version and the technical details of the crash. Reports contain no photos, vacation names or other content. You can turn this off in Settings → Crash reports.

What we don't do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of personal data.

Sharing

When you share a collage, it goes to the app you pick (for example a messenger) and is handled under that app's policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
