My Vacation

Questions, bugs or ideas: ceoolik@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the app version from Settings. You can also use Settings → Report a problem in the app.

Common questions

Can I see a photo before the vacation ends? No, that's the point: every photo is sealed when you take it. Use Finish early to open everything now.

I didn't get a reminder. Check that notifications are allowed for My Vacation in the system settings. On some Android phones, battery saving can delay them.

Will I lose my photos if I change phones? On iPhone they move with your iCloud or computer backup. On Android, use the phone-to-phone transfer when setting up the new phone. With My Vacation+, Cloud Backup restores them on any phone with your recovery key.

How do I cancel My Vacation+ or get a refund? Cancel in your store account: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. Refunds are made by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google (Google Play refunds). See Terms, section 4.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Manage account → Delete account, or see the Privacy Policy if you no longer have the phone.

Report a photo, a link or a person

In a group trip: open the photo or the person, then Report or Block. On a shared link page: Report at the bottom. Or write to ceoolik@gmail.com with the subject "My Vacation report" and the link or the group's name. We aim to review reports within 24 hours. Content that sexualises a child is removed and reported to NCMEC. If someone is in danger right now, call your local emergency number first. How notices, decisions and appeals work: Terms, sections 7–8.

Authorities: send legal requests to the same address with the subject "Law enforcement request".
