My Vacation

Questions, bugs or ideas: ceoolik@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the app version from Settings.

Common questions

Can I see a photo before the vacation ends? No, that's the point: every photo is sealed when you take it. Use Finish early to open everything now.

I didn't get a reminder. Check that notifications are allowed for My Vacation in the system settings. On some Android phones, battery saving can delay them.

Will I lose my photos if I change phones? On iPhone they move with your iCloud or computer backup. On Android, use the phone-to-phone transfer when setting up the new phone.

Report a photo, a link or a person

In a group trip: open the photo or the person, then Report or Block. On a shared link page: Report at the bottom. Or write to ceoolik@gmail.com with the subject "My Vacation report" and the link or the group's name. We review reports within 24 hours. Content that sexualises a child is removed and reported to NCMEC. If someone is in danger right now, call your local emergency number first.

Authorities: send legal requests to the same address with the subject "Law enforcement request".
