Reporting. In a group, open a photo or a person and tap Report, or Block to hide someone from you. On a shared link page, use Report at the bottom. You can also write to ceoolik@gmail.com with the subject "My Vacation report"; include the link or the group's name. Because photos are encrypted, we can only check a photo if the report includes the link or a copy of the photo; the app asks before sending one. We review reports within 24 hours and illegal content first.

A link that gets several reports in a short time is paused until we check it. If you think we got it wrong, reply to our message or write to the address above and a person will look again.
